Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Tools ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features and services enabled in the app, we may process the following categories of information:

  • PDFs and images: Documents selected by you for viewing, compression, conversion, merging, splitting, or other PDF operations.
  • Usage and analytics data: App interactions, feature usage, performance information, diagnostics, and crash reports, where analytics or diagnostic services are enabled.
  • Device information: Certain device identifiers, app version, operating system details, and technical information may be processed by integrated SDKs.
  • Purchase information: If premium subscriptions are introduced, subscription status, product identifiers, transaction-related information, and purchase verification data may be processed through Google Play or other configured billing services.
  • Support information: Your email address and the information you provide when contacting support.

2. PDF Files and Local Processing

PDF Tools is designed to process selected documents on your device wherever the feature supports local processing. The app accesses files you select or authorize through Android's file picker and related storage APIs.

Unless a feature explicitly explains otherwise, we do not intend to upload the contents of your documents to our own servers. However, you should verify the behavior of each feature and any third-party service before using the app with confidential files.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use analytics and diagnostic services to understand app usage, identify crashes, improve performance, and fix technical issues. These services may process events, device or app information, and diagnostic data according to their own policies and the configuration used in the app.

Before release, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated to identify the analytics and diagnostics providers actually integrated into the app.

4. Advertising

The app may offer advertising, including through Google AdMob or other advertising providers, if these services are enabled in a released version. Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, or other data permitted by their SDK and settings.

Personalized advertising, consent, and opt-out options will be handled as required by applicable laws and the relevant platform policies. Please review the privacy policy of each advertising provider used by the app.

5. Premium Subscription and Payments

PDF Tools may introduce paid features or premium subscriptions in the future. If introduced, purchases may be processed through Google Play Billing or another clearly identified payment provider.

Payment card details are generally handled by the payment provider rather than directly by PDF Tools. We may receive purchase tokens, product information, subscription status, and transaction verification details needed to activate, maintain, or restore premium access.

Subscription prices, billing periods, renewals, cancellation instructions, and applicable terms will be displayed through the relevant purchase interface. Users can generally manage Google Play subscriptions through their Google Play account settings.

6. Permissions

The app may use Android's system file picker, document access APIs, and other permissions required by specific features. Permissions are used to provide the requested functionality. You can manage available permissions through your device settings.

7. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain app features.
  • Improve performance and troubleshoot errors.
  • Understand feature usage and improve user experience.
  • Display and measure advertising, where enabled.
  • Process, verify, and restore premium subscriptions, where enabled.
  • Respond to support requests and address misuse or security issues.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell the contents of your PDF documents. Information may be processed by service providers that support app analytics, diagnostics, advertising, payment processing, security, or hosting, where those services are integrated and necessary.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security. The actual third-party services used must be identified in the final published policy and relevant store disclosures.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Files saved to shared storage may remain on your device until you delete them. App-private information may be deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app, although some third-party services may retain their own records according to their retention policies.

For requests relating to information associated with support communications or any future account feature, contact us using the email below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction purposes.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device secure and protect passwords used for encrypted documents.

11. Children's Privacy

PDF Tools is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Third-Party Services

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and data practices. Depending on the services included in the released app, relevant policies may include:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics information
  • Google advertising technologies
  • Google Play subscriptions

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
