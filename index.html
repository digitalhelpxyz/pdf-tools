About PDF Tools

PDF Tools is an Android utility app designed to make everyday PDF tasks simple and convenient. Our goal is to bring useful document tools together in one easy-to-use application.

Our Features

  • PDF Viewer
  • PDF Compression
  • PDF to JPG or PNG conversion
  • Images to PDF conversion
  • PDF merging and splitting
  • PDF password protection and password removal for authorized documents
  • PDF page organization and other supported document utilities
  • Additional conversion features as supported by the app version

Simple Document Management

PDF Tools aims to provide a clear interface, helpful progress indicators, and convenient ways to save or share processed files. Actual feature availability may vary by version and device.

Conversion Quality

Some document conversions, especially PDF to editable Word or Excel formats, may not preserve every font, table, image, or page layout exactly. Please review converted documents before using them for important purposes.

Privacy

We aim to process files locally wherever supported. Analytics, diagnostics, advertising, and subscription services may be used if included in the installed version. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
